Quick answers to common visitor questions regarding conservation areas, logistics, and planning.

Start with the featured destinations on Home. Select View destination for an overview, things to do, and practical visitor information on River No. 2 Beach, Banana Islands, and Tiwai Island Sanctuary.

River No. 2 Beach Banana Islands Tiwai Island Gola Rainforest

Visit Services to explore tour operator support. Confirm itineraries, availability and arrangements directly with your chosen registered provider. All certified operators undergo regular safety and sustainability audits by the National Tourist Board.

Explore tour operators on Services arrow_forward

Use the accommodation section on Services as a starting point. Check current facilities and availability before booking coastal lodges, peninsula guesthouses, or eco-safari treehouse camps. Many community-run beach chalets offer walk-in or telephone reservations.

Send your interests and questions using the form above. Check current travel guidance, seasonal ocean tide charts, and local conditions as part of your planning. Our concierge team can recommend tailored multi-day loops combining Freetown's historical sites with untouched peninsula beaches.
