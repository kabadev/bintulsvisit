## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an editorial luxury narrative for ecotourism, nature exploration, and heritage storytelling. It pairs the timeless warmth of literary culture with hyper-modern, clean structural interface design.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact

- **Timeless Authority & Discovery**: Elicits a sense of curated expedition, untamed Atlantic coastlines, and ancient rainforest canopies.
- **Uncluttered Sophistication**: Eliminates superficial ornamentation, faux bevels, borders, and drop shadows in favor of pure form, organic contrast, and expansive negative space.
- **Warm Welcome**: Replaces stark digital whites with organic paper-tone surfaces, inviting contemplative reading and exploration.

### Visual Style

A deliberate synthesis of **Heritage Editorial** and **Flat Ultra-Rounded Modernism**. High-contrast, sweeping serif display headings evoke prestige travel volumes, while spacious, borderless containers with pill-shaped accents maintain contemporary digital velocity and effortless usability.

## Colors

The palette is derived from coastal rainforests, soft sands, sea mist, and vibrant West African textile accents. Contrast is achieved through distinct tonal layering rather than line art or shadows.

### Primary & Functional Roles

- **Primary Emerald (`#15803d`)**: The deep vegetative anchor. Used for primary call-to-action fills, dominant badge backgrounds, and primary navigation states.
- **Secondary Mint (`#80ffcc`)**: High-energy coastal luminescence. Applied to micro-highlights, live availability indicators, active badges, and interactive accent fills.
- **Tertiary Warm Peach (`#ff9b8a`)**: Sundown accent. Used sparingly for curated heritage highlights, cultural tags, and notification callouts.
- **Supporting Accents**:
  - Sky Blue (`#9edcff`): Water excursions, marine conservation, climate metrics.
  - Lilac (`#cdbdff`): Arts, artisanal crafts, nocturnal cultural guides.
  - Warm Sun (`#ffe58f`): Seasonal recommendations, alerts, community events.

### Surfaces & Backgrounds

- **Primary Canvas Background (`#e9ecea`)**: A muted, natural grey-green substrate that sets off content blocks seamlessly.
- **Surface / Paper (`#f7f7f2`)**: Warm, breathable surface for content wells, headers, and neutral containers.
- **Pure White Cards (`#ffffff`)**: Borderless, shadowless focal islands sitting atop `#e9ecea` to create instant visual structure.

### Text & Ink Hierarchy

- **Primary Ink (`#202322`)**: Deep charcoal forest black for headlines and primary body text.
- **Secondary Charcoal (`#3d3d3d`)**: Sub-headers, secondary metadata, and icon fills.
- **Muted Sage (`#747a77`)**: Captions, timestamps, disabled labels, and quiet navigational elements.

## Typography

The type system blends the classical elegance of `Manrope` with the clean, rhythmic geometry of `Manrope`.

### Display & Editorial Hierarchy

- **Manrope** is reserved for high-impact story arcs, destination titles, collection headers, and quote callouts. It gives an immediate feel of literary heritage, cultural gravitas, and luxury exploration.
- Ensure italic variants of `Manrope` are deployed on select thematic terms within large headers (e.g., _The Peninsula_, _Sanctuary_) to instill crafted editorial nuance.

### Functional System Typography

- **Manrope** drives all operational UI: interface labels, itinerary listings, card metadata, and long-form narrative body copy.
- Generous line heights ensure effortless legibility across travel guides and comprehensive booking overviews.
- Body copy uses `Manrope Regular` (400) at 15px/18px to prevent visual fatigue, while labels and data indicators leverage `Manrope SemiBold` (600) and `Bold` (700) with slight positive tracking for crisp utility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an expansive 12-column fluid grid system that prioritizes rhythmic whitespace, natural eye movement, and large photographic viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoints

- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12 columns, 24px gutters (`gutter`), 48px outer margins (`margin`), max container width of 1440px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px outer margins (`margin-tablet`).
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4 columns, 16px gutters (`gutter-mobile`), 20px outer margins (`margin-mobile`).

### Spacing Philosophy

- White cards and surface panels float cleanly on the `#e9ecea` background canvas with minimum separation of `space-md` (16px) on mobile and `space-lg` to `space-xl` (24px to 40px) on desktop.
- Generous internal padding (24px–36px) within content containers prevents text and media from crowding card perimeters, preserving the open-air luxury atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth

This system operates under a strict **Zero-Elevation, Zero-Stroke** framework. Visual separation is accomplished entirely through pure color blocking and geometric contrast.

### Pure Tonal Layering

- **No Box Shadows**: Absolutely no drop shadows, directional shadows, or ambient glows are permitted across any component or modal layer.
- **No Borders or Outlines**: Containers never use border strokes to carve boundaries. Surface definition is derived strictly from the contrast between `#ffffff` or `#f7f7f2` blocks resting directly over `#e9ecea`.
- **Z-Index & Overlays**: Modals, slide-overs, and floating navigation bars rely on deep color contrast (e.g., solid `#202322` drawers or `#ffffff` panels over a translucent deep ink backdrop of `rgba(32, 35, 34, 0.4)` without backdrop blur or shadow halos).
- **Interactive Depth**: Hover and focus states never lift elements on the Z-axis. Instead, interaction is communicated through swift color transitions, subtle background tints, or scale transforms on imagery within fixed rounded containers.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a prominent, organic pill-shaped geometric language that softens high-density travel data and harmonizes with natural coastal topography.

### Shape Hierarchy

- **Pill (Fully Rounded / 9999px)**: Interactive controls, primary buttons, pill tags, search inputs, icon wrappers, and badge chips.
- **Extra Large Radius (3rem / 48px – 32px)**: Primary destination feature cards, hero image frame bounds, and structural section backdrops.
- **Large Radius (2rem / 32px – 22px)**: Standard content cards, itinerary breakdown panels, media carousels, and dialog windows.
- **Inner Nested Radius**: When nesting elements within larger containers, internal cards or images maintain proportional curvature (20px nested inside 32px containers) to sustain visual harmony without corner tension.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Button**: Solid fill in `#15803d` with white text (`#ffffff`), fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-md` horizontal padding (24px), and 14px `Manrope` bold typography. On hover: shifts background smoothly to `#16a34a`.
- **Secondary Button**: Solid fill in `#f7f7f2` with `#202322` text. On hover: transitions to `#80ffcc` with dark ink text.
- **Tertiary Accent Button**: `#80ffcc` fill with `#202322` text for high-impact booking actions.
- **Rules**: Strictly zero border strokes and zero box-shadows.

### Chips & Badges

- **Editorial Badges**: Pill-shaped containers with 6px vertical and 14px horizontal padding.
- **Color Pairings**:
  - Nature / Ecotourism: Mint background (`#80ffcc`) with Deep Ink text (`#202322`).
  - Heritage / History: Warm Peach background (`#ff9b8a`) with Deep Ink text (`#202322`).
  - Marine / Water: Sky Blue background (`#9edcff`) with Deep Ink text (`#202322`).
  - Wildlife / Culture: Lilac background (`#cdbdff`) with Deep Ink text (`#202322`).

### Cards & Feature Tiles

- **Standard Card**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) background, large corner radius (32px), internal padding of 24px to 32px. No borders, no shadows.
- **Media Card**: Photography fills the top or background with clipped 32px corners. Metadata rests in an integrated `#ffffff` or `#f7f7f2` inner block.
- **Tonal Well**: Background `#f7f7f2` used for secondary summaries, itinerary day breakdowns, and transport specs.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields

- **Search Bar**: Full pill shape with a pure white (`#ffffff`) background, minimum height of 56px, containing deep charcoal text (`#202322`) and placeholder text in `#747a77`.
- **Text Fields**: Soft `#f7f7f2` fill, 22px to pill radius, 16px vertical padding, no border lines.
- **Focus States**: High-contrast subtle background shift to `#ffffff` and a delicate, un-blurred 2px focus ring tinted in `#15803d` offset by 2px for accessibility without heavy styling.

### Selection Controls

- **Checkboxes**: 22px square with an ultra-soft 8px radius, solid `#f7f7f2` background in default state; fills solid `#15803d` with a crisp white checkmark when selected. No border lines.
- **Radio Buttons**: Pill circular base, active state fills solid `#15803d` with a centered 8px `#80ffcc` dot.

### Specialized Travel Components

- **Curated Itinerary Step**: Vertical layout on `#ffffff` surfaces connected by solid 2px `#e9ecea` track lines without drop shadows; step numbers enclosed in circular mint `#80ffcc` pills.
- **Stat & Weather Island**: Ultra-rounded (24px) `#f7f7f2` mini-containers highlighting seasonal temperatures, swell conditions, and biodiversity metrics using large `Manrope` numerical figures paired with compact `Manrope` labels.
